4x-speed-mp4-player - installation notes
=========================================

This app is not code-signed. The first launch may show a warning.

macOS (dmg, Apple silicon, M1 or later)
  1. Open the .dmg and drag the app into Applications.
  2. The first launch is blocked. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     then press "Open Anyway" for this app and confirm.

Windows (NSIS installer, x64)
  1. Run the installer. SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC".
  2. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Linux (AppImage, x64 or arm64)
  1. Make the file executable:   chmod +x 4x-speed-mp4-player-*.AppImage
  2. Start it with a double-click or from a terminal.
